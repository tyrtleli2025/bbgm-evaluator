Player availability

Injuries are fixed for the entire series; players do not heal between games. Minor injuries (1–4 games remaining) use BBGM’s default playoff policy and reduce ratings by 2.5% per remaining game. “Out for series” removes the player from the rotation. New injuries are disabled.
